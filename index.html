About Leela Residency

A quiet home away from home.

Tucked into Ariyanattu, 2nd South Street, Leela Residency is a boutique guest house made for pilgrims and families visiting Velankanni. Enjoy clean air-conditioned rooms, a private entrance, kitchen access and a peaceful terrace, all with warm, personal hospitality.
